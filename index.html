💡 Pro Trading Tips

▲
  • Cut losses early: Don't let a 5% drop become a 20% drop. Plan your exit before you enter.
  • Check Volatility: A high Strength score is significantly safer when the Volatility score is low.
  • Trade with the Trend: "The trend is your friend." Avoid "catching a falling knife" (buying stocks in a steep downtrend).
  • Position Sizing: Never put more than 10% of your total capital into a single trade to manage risk.
  • Understand Settlement: Be mindful of T+1 or T+2 settlement rules to avoid Good Faith Violations.
  • Watch the Spread: On low-volume stocks, the gap between Bid and Ask can be huge—use Limit Orders.
